This is the public contact page for GiftLoop.

Contact

Email essentialtradecontractors@gmail.com. Essential Trade Contractors LLC, 130 County Road, Dennis, NJ 08210. Signed-in members can also send a note from Account.

What we can help with

Sign-in and confirmation email, an invitation that will not accept, a draw that will not run, a reminder that did not arrive, and deleting an account. We do not look up who someone is giving a gift to.

Shopping

Store links may include a referral code. GiftLoop does not sell digital goods and does not take payment inside the app. A purchase happens on the retailer’s site, in the browser.
